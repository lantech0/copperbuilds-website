## The honest truth about local SEO

Google doesn't rank new websites instantly. It takes time for Google to crawl your site, index your pages, and start trusting your business as a real, established local provider. That's not a flaw — it's how local SEO has always worked, and every legitimate agency will tell you the same thing.

What you *will* see in the first 30 days: your site live, your Google Business Profile optimized, and the foundations in place. What builds from there — traffic, calls, leads — follows a predictable arc over 90 days.

Here's exactly what to expect.

---

## Day 1–30 — Foundation

**What we're doing:**

- [ ] Your website is live and loading fast on mobile and desktop
- [ ] Google Analytics and Google Search Console are connected — we can see how Google sees your site
- [ ] Your Google Business Profile is fully optimized: photos, services, hours, description, and linked to your website
- [ ] Your first 4 Google Business Profile posts are scheduled (one per week)
- [ ] We've started submitting your business to the top local directories (Yelp, BBB, Apple Maps, Bing Places, and more) to build your citation footprint
- [ ] Your review request workflow is set up — customers start getting follow-up messages after each job

**What you'll see:**

- Your site appearing in Google search results for your business name within 3–7 days
- Your GBP profile showing up in Google Maps within the first week
- Google Search Console showing your first impressions (people seeing your site in results) — even if they're not clicking yet, this is Google starting to notice you
- Your first monthly report delivered — this is your baseline

**What you won't see yet:**

Organic traffic from people searching for your service who don't know your name. That comes in Month 2 and Month 3. Don't judge the investment by Month 1 numbers — we're planting seeds.

## Day 31–60 — Google Starts Noticing

**What we're doing:**

- [ ] Citations continue: 2 new directories verified or claimed each week
- [ ] Weekly GBP posts continue — job spotlights, seasonal offers, local tips
- [ ] Review responses: every new review gets a personal reply within 48 hours
- [ ] Review velocity tracked: are new reviews coming in? If not, we escalate the review request workflow
- [ ] Your keyword rankings are monitored — we check where you appear for "[your service] [your city]" and related terms

**Lead Machine and Market Leader, additionally:**

- [ ] **Blog post #1 and #2 published** — targeting questions your customers actually search (e.g., "How much does [service] cost in [city]?", "Signs you need [service]")
- [ ] Link building begins: reaching out to local directories, trade associations, and community sites to get your site linked from other credible local sources
- [ ] Competitor check: we review the top 3 competitors in your market — are they gaining or losing ground?

**What you'll see:**

- Your GBP profile getting views and clicks — Google Maps is starting to show your business to people nearby
- First phone calls from people who found you on Google Maps (not just people who already knew your name)
- Keyword rankings starting to appear in Search Console — you may be on page 2 or 3 for your primary keyword. That's normal and expected at this stage.
- Your second monthly report with Month 1 vs. Month 2 comparison

**The milestone to watch in Month 2:**

> "Is Google showing your business to people who don't already know you?" If the answer is yes — even a handful of impressions — the machine is working.

## What "success" looks like at 90 days

Every market is different, but here's what a healthy 90-day result looks like for a home service business:

| Signal | Healthy at 90 days |
|--------|-------------------|
| GBP profile views | 200–600/month (depending on market size) |
| Website clicks from GBP | 30–100+/month |
| Calls from GBP | 10–40+/month |
| Google review count | 10+ new reviews since launch |
| Target keywords ranking | Appearing in top 20 for 3–5 primary keywords |
| Organic search clicks | 20–80+/month (low for new sites — will grow) |

If your numbers are in this range at 90 days, the foundation is working. Months 4–6 are typically when the compounding effect becomes obvious — traffic starts to accelerate because you have content, citations, reviews, and backlinks stacking on each other.
