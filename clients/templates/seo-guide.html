Section 1

What is SEO, really?

SEO stands for "Search Engine Optimization" — but ignore the technical name. Here's what it actually means for your business:

🔍

Someone searches for your service

"Plumber near me" · "AC repair Tampa" · "Emergency electrician" — these are the searches that matter.

📋

Google shows them a list of businesses

Google picks the businesses it trusts most to answer that search — and puts them at the top.

📞

They call whoever shows up first

The #1 result gets roughly 5× more calls than #5. Position matters — a lot.

💡

The simple version:

SEO is everything we do to make Google trust your business enough to show it to people who are actively looking to hire someone in your trade, in your city, right now.

Section 2

The two places customers find you on Google

When someone searches for your trade, Google shows two different types of results. We work on both.

Where most calls come from

The Google Maps Pack

The box with 3 local businesses that appears at the top of Google search results — right above the regular website links. When someone searches "plumber near me," this is what they see first.

Why it matters:

Most people tap one of these 3 businesses and never scroll down. Getting into this box is the single biggest move for a home services business.

Builds long-term traffic

Organic Website Results

The regular blue links below the Maps box. These are websites Google trusts to answer specific searches — like "how much does a water heater replacement cost in Tampa?"

Why it matters:

People searching these terms are researching before they call. A helpful article on your site builds trust and often leads to a call days or weeks later.

The key difference: Google Maps results are based on your business profile and reviews. Organic website results are based on your website content and authority. We manage both — because your competitors are working on both.

Section 3

How Google decides who shows up first

Google doesn't pick randomly. It looks at three things — think of them as the three legs of a stool. All three need to be strong for your business to rank well.

Leg 1

Relevance

Does your website clearly explain what you do and where you do it? Google needs to see "plumber in Tampa" — not just "quality service."

What we do: Write your pages to target the exact phrases your customers search for, in the cities you serve.
Leg 2

Trust

Does Google trust your business? Trust comes from reviews, how long you've been online, how many other websites link to yours, and accurate directory listings.

What we do: Build citations (directory listings), earn reviews, and get links from credible local and industry sites.
Leg 3

Activity

Is your business active? Google favors businesses that post on their Google profile, respond to reviews, and add new content. Dormant businesses drop in rankings.

What we do: Post weekly on your Google Business Profile, respond to all reviews, and publish regular articles on your site.
⚖️

The honest truth about timing: Building all three legs takes time. Relevance is set up in the first month. Trust builds over 3–6 months as reviews accumulate and citations spread. Activity compounds — the longer we keep it up, the stronger it gets. There are no shortcuts that last.

Section 5

What to expect — and when

SEO is not instant. Here's an honest, month-by-month picture of what happens and when you should start seeing results.

Month 1
The Foundation
Your site goes live. Google Business Profile is fully set up. Analytics and Search Console are connected. Citations start going out. Review requests begin. You won't see organic traffic yet — Google is still learning your site exists. What you will see: your business name appearing in Google results, your GBP showing on Maps, and your first monthly report as a baseline.
Months 2–3
Google Starts Noticing
Your GBP profile starts getting views and clicks. The first calls from people who didn't already know your name arrive — they found you on Google Maps. Keyword rankings begin appearing: you may be on page 2 or 3 for your main terms. That's normal and expected. The milestone to watch: is Google showing your business to people who don't already know you? Even a handful of new impressions means the machine is working.
Months 4–6
Traction Builds
Content, citations, reviews, and links are stacking on each other. Page 1 appearances start for some of your target keywords — typically the longer, more specific ones first ("emergency plumber Tampa" before "plumber Tampa"). Review count hits 10+ which meaningfully improves Maps visibility. Organic calls and contact form submissions start arriving from people who had never heard of you before.
Month 6+
Compounding Returns
This is where local SEO pays its biggest dividends. Each month of consistent activity compounds the months before it. Businesses that stay consistent through the first 6 months typically see ranking velocity accelerate — not plateau — in months 7–12. The businesses that quit at Month 3 because "it wasn't working yet" are the ones you'll outrank.
📅

Healthy 90-day benchmarks. GBP profile views: 200–600/month · Website clicks from GBP: 30–100+/month · Calls from GBP: 10–40+/month · New reviews since launch: 10+ · Target keywords in top 20: 3–5. Every market is different — we'll show you your own numbers each month and explain what they mean.

How to read your monthly report

Every month you get a report. Here's what each number means — in plain English.

Impressions
How many times Google showed your business in search results — whether someone clicked or not. Think of this as your visibility. It grows first, before clicks do.
187
Clicks
How many people clicked on your website from a Google search result. A growing click number means Google is showing you for searches where people actually want what you offer.
6.4
Average Position
Your average rank across all search terms. Position 1–3 = top of page 1. Position 4–10 = page 1 but lower. Position 11+ = page 2 or beyond. Lower number = better rank.

GBP Calls
Phone calls that came directly from your Google Business Profile listing. These are people who found your business on Google Maps and called you — not referrals, not repeat customers.

How this month compares to last month. Early months show big swings because the numbers are small — a jump from 10 to 15 clicks is +50%. Focus on the trend direction, not just the percentage.

Section 7

What we need from you — every month

We handle the strategy, posting, and reporting. But there are three things that make the work noticeably stronger — and only you can provide them.

📸

2–4 job site photos, any month you have them

Phone photos are fine. Before/after shots are great. Real job photos outperform stock images on Google Business Profile every time — Google's algorithm and your customers both respond to them. Text them or send them via email whenever you finish a job you're proud of.

⭐

A heads-up if the review request workflow isn't running

After every completed job, your customers get a review request via email or text. If you're not seeing new reviews most months, let us know — something in the workflow may need adjusting. Reviews are too important to let sit unchecked. Ten reviews in 90 days changes your Maps visibility meaningfully.

📢

Any business updates — the moment they happen

New phone number. New service area. Changed hours. A seasonal promotion. A new service you're now offering. These need to go on your Google Business Profile the same week they happen — inconsistent info across the web hurts your rankings. Just email us and we'll update everything within 48 hours.

The more specific you give us, the more specific we get. Your photos and job stories become next month's GBP posts and blog content. A generic "completed a roofing job" becomes a weak post. "We replaced a 22-year-old shingle roof in South Tampa after Hurricane Helene damage — here's how we matched the original tile color" becomes a post that actually converts.
