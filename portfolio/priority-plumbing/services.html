Leak Detection Tampa FL

Electronic Leak
Detection Service

A water bill that keeps climbing, damp spots on walls or ceilings, or the sound of running water with everything off — these are signs of a hidden leak. We use acoustic listening equipment and thermal imaging to locate leaks behind walls, under slabs, and beneath floors without opening up your entire home to find them.

Most leak locations are found within 30–60 minutes. We tell you exactly where it is before recommending any repair — so you're not paying to tear up flooring on a guess.

What's Included

  • Acoustic leak detection (behind walls and under floors)
  • Under-slab leak location
  • Thermal imaging for temperature-differential detection
  • Water meter test and pressure testing
  • Written leak location report before repair begins

Common Questions

Tampa Plumbing FAQ

How much does a plumber cost in Tampa FL?

Most repairs range from $150–$600 depending on the job type and parts needed. Emergency after-hours calls may include a dispatch fee. We provide a written flat-rate quote before starting any work — the price you're quoted is the price you pay.

How much does water heater replacement cost?

Tank replacements typically run $800–$1,800 installed in Tampa, depending on tank size and fuel type. Tankless (on-demand) units range from $1,500–$3,000 installed. We carry common units on our trucks — most jobs are completed same-day.

What plumbing emergencies need immediate attention?

Call immediately for: burst or ruptured pipes, sewage backup into the home, water that won't shut off, no water pressure without explanation, or any active leak spreading through walls or flooring. Waiting even a few hours can add thousands in water damage.

How do you unclog a drain in Tampa?

For minor clogs, a plunger can help. For recurring or stubborn blockages — especially grease buildup or root intrusion — professional hydro-jetting is the most effective solution. We clear most drains the same day and diagnose root causes so the problem doesn't keep coming back.
