Common Questions

Roof Questions — Straight Answers

How much does a new roof cost in Tampa FL?

A full residential roof replacement in Tampa ranges from $8,000–$18,000 depending on square footage, roof pitch, and material. We provide free detailed written estimates — you'll know exactly what you're paying for before we start.

Does homeowners insurance cover roof replacement in Florida?

In most cases, yes — storm, wind, and hail damage are covered by standard Florida homeowners policies. Age-related deterioration typically is not. We work directly with adjusters and help document your claim properly.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements take 1–3 days. We pull permits, handle the county inspection, and don't consider the job finished until the inspection passes and your yard is clean.

What's the best roofing material for Tampa FL?

Architectural asphalt shingles are the most common and cost-effective for Tampa's climate. Metal roofing lasts longer and handles heat better but costs more upfront. Tile is popular in certain neighborhoods and extremely durable. We'll recommend what makes sense for your home and budget.
