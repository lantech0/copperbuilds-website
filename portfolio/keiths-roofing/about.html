Our Story

20 Years. One Name. One Standard.

Keith started roofing in his early 20s, working crew for a larger company across Pinellas County. By 2005, he had his license and his first truck. The pitch was simple: show up when you say you will, do the work right, and clean up completely when you leave.

Two decades later, most of his business still comes from referrals. He's replaced roofs for the children of people whose roofs he replaced in 2008. That's what "Quality Through The Roof" actually means — not a slogan on a sign, but a reputation built one job at a time.

Keith does not subcontract. Every job is done by his own licensed, insured crew. He's on every job site. That's how he knows the work is done right.
