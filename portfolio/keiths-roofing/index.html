Common Questions

Before You Call

How much does roof repair cost in Tampa FL?

Most repairs range from $300–$1,500 depending on damage type and scope. We provide a free written estimate before any work begins — no surprises on your bill.

Does homeowners insurance cover roof repair in Florida?

In most cases, yes. Storm damage, wind, and hail are covered by standard Florida policies. We work with adjusters and help document your claim at no extra charge.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements are completed in 1–2 days. Larger or complex roofs may take 3–4 days. We give you a specific timeline in your written estimate before scheduling.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. Every job is performed by our own licensed crew — not a subcontracted team we've never worked with before. That's how we maintain consistent quality on every project.
