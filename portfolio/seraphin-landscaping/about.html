Our Story

From One Mower to Tampa Bay's Trusted Name

Jasmine Seraphin started this company with a single lawn mower, a pickup truck, and a conviction that Tampa Bay homeowners deserved a lawn care company that actually showed up, did the work right, and treated their property like it was their own.

Seven years later, that conviction hasn't changed. What has changed: a full crew of trained professionals, a fleet of commercial equipment, and hundreds of families across Hillsborough County who wouldn't trust their lawn to anyone else.

Being woman-owned isn't a marketing angle for us — it's a reflection of how we run the business. We communicate clearly. We respect your time. We don't disappear after the first job. You'll always know who's coming to your property, when they're arriving, and exactly what was done.

What We Stand For

Our Values Run Deep

We built this company around four beliefs — and they show up in every job we do, from a weekly mow to a full landscape renovation.

🤝

Reliability First

If we say Tuesday, we're there Tuesday. If something changes, you hear from us before your lawn does. Reliability is the most underrated quality in this industry — and the one we protect hardest.

🌿

Craft Over Speed

We don't rush a job to fit more in the truck. Edging gets done right. Blow-down gets done after every single cut. We take the extra few minutes because that's the difference between okay and excellent.

💬

Clear Communication

You always know what we're doing and why. Estimates are written, detailed, and explained before work starts. No surprise invoices. No "we'll figure it out later." Straight talk from day one.

🏡

Your Property, Our Pride

We treat every lawn like it's in front of our own home. The crew knows that standard. Our repeat clients notice the difference — and that's why more than 80% of our new business comes from referrals.
