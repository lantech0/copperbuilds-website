Common Questions

Concrete Questions,
Straight Answers.

A standard concrete driveway in Tampa typically costs $6–$12 per square foot depending on thickness, finish, and site prep. Decorative stamped concrete runs $12–$20/sqft. We provide free on-site written estimates for every project.
Permits are required for foundations, large slabs, and work that affects drainage or structural elements. Steelman handles all permit applications as part of the project — no extra charge, no permit office headaches.
Most residential driveways are poured in one day. We allow 24–48 hours before light foot traffic and 7 days before vehicles. We apply sealer after proper curing — never early — and do a final walkthrough before we leave.
All concrete cracks eventually — the goal is to control where it cracks. We cut expansion joints at proper intervals, compact the sub-base correctly, and use the right mix design for Florida's conditions. This is what separates a 30-year slab from a 5-year slab.
Yes. We do crack repair, surface grinding, and full concrete resurfacing for driveways and slabs that have settled, heaved, or cracked. We'll tell you honestly whether repair or replacement is the smarter investment for your situation.
Yes — footings, monolithic slabs, stem walls, and floating foundations for garages, additions, workshops, and new construction. We work alongside builders and GCs across Tampa Bay and handle all structural concrete from dig to pour.
