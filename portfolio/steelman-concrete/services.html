Driveways &
Aprons

A concrete driveway is one of the highest-ROI upgrades a homeowner can make. We size the slab correctly, prep the base to spec, and finish it to hold up under Florida's heat cycles and afternoon downpours for decades.

  • Standard 4" residential slab (6" available for trucks/RVs)
  • Rebar or fiber reinforcement included
  • Expansion joints cut to prevent cracking
  • Broom finish, trowel finish, or decorative exposed aggregate
  • Aprons and turn-arounds included where needed
  • Permit pulled and inspected before pour

Common Questions

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

How much per square foot in Tampa?

Standard brushed concrete runs $6–$12/sqft. Stamped runs $12–$20/sqft. Actual cost depends on thickness, sub-base condition, and finish — we quote after seeing the site, not before.

How long does concrete last in Florida?

A properly installed driveway lasts 25–40 years. Florida's wet summers mean sub-base prep and joint placement are critical. Shortcuts here cause cracks in under 5 years — we don't take them.

Is stamped concrete worth the extra cost?

For pool decks and front entries — yes, usually. It's 2–3x the cost of plain concrete but adds significant curb appeal. We'll show you samples on-site and let you decide with no pressure.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We pull every required Hillsborough County permit, schedule inspections, and handle all the paperwork. It's included in the project cost — no extra charge to you.
