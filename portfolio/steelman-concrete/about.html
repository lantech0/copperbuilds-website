The Story

Frank Steelman,
Owner

Frank Steelman grew up watching his father finish driveways in Hillsborough County. By 18 he was on the crew. By 24 he was running jobs. In 2002, he started Steelman Concrete out of his garage with two employees and a referral from a neighbor — a driveway on Habana Ave that's still in perfect shape today.

Twenty-two years later, the crew has grown to 14, the license wall has more certifications on it, and the work ethic hasn't changed. Frank still shows up to every estimate personally. He still walks the job the morning after the pour.

The reason Steelman doesn't advertise much is simple: most of the work comes from the same neighborhoods where previous jobs were done. That kind of referral business only happens when the concrete is laid right.

How We Work

Four Rules
We Don't Break

01
No Shortcuts on Prep

Sub-base prep is invisible after the pour. That's why some contractors skip it. We don't. If the base fails, the slab fails — it's that simple.

02
Written Quotes Only

Every estimate is written, itemized, and signed. Nothing verbal, nothing approximate. You know exactly what you're getting before work starts.

License Verification

Common
Questions

Is Steelman Concrete licensed in Florida?

Yes. Florida Certified General Contractor license CBC1262809. You can verify this at myfloridalicense.com at any time — we encourage you to check.

How long have you been in business?

Since 2002 — over 22 years of continuous operation in Hillsborough County and the greater Tampa Bay area, with 3,200+ projects completed.

Do you use subcontractors?

Our concrete crews are direct employees — not subs. We control the quality on-site. We may use licensed subcontractors for electrical, plumbing, or survey work when a project requires it.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We warranty all concrete work for 1 year against defects in workmanship. Cracks caused by sub-base settlement or tree root intrusion are outside the warranty scope — we'll tell you upfront if we see risk factors.
