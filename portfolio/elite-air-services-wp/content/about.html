Our Story

15 Years Keeping Tampa Cool

Elite Air Services started in 2010 with one truck, one technician, and a simple idea: Tampa homeowners deserve an HVAC company that doesn't treat them like a commission check.

Founder Christian Reyes spent eight years as a field tech for a large regional HVAC chain — long enough to see how the big shops work. Oversized quotes, upsold parts, and technicians paid on commission. He started Elite Air to do the opposite.

Today, Elite Air Services has six trucks on the road, serves all of Hillsborough County, and maintains a 4.9-star Google rating across 200+ reviews. Every technician is background-checked, state-licensed, and paid a flat salary — not a cut of what they sell you.
