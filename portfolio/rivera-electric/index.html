About Rivera Electric

Started by a Tampa
Electrician. Still Run by One.

Marco Rivera started Rivera Electric in 2007 after spending a decade as a licensed journeyman for commercial contractors. He wanted to build something where he knew every customer by name and where the quality didn't slip because someone was rushing a job to make the next one.

The company has grown to a four-person crew — all licensed journeymen — and hasn't changed its model. Marco is still on most jobs. Every permit is pulled before the work starts. Every inspection is passed before the panel is closed up.
