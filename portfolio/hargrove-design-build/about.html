James Hargrove — Founder

Built on craft.
Grown on referrals.

James Hargrove spent a decade as a finish carpenter before starting this company. He'd seen every shortcut contractors take — the ones that look fine on walkthrough day and show up as problems two years later. He started Hargrove in 2010 because he wanted to build renovations the way he'd want his own home built.

In the first year, the company completed four projects — all kitchens, all South Tampa, all by word-of-mouth. Fifteen years later, the company has grown to 12 employees and over 120 completed renovations, but the referral rate hasn't changed. Roughly 80% of new clients come from someone who watched us work in a neighbor's kitchen or bathroom.

James still walks every job site weekly. He still does the in-home consultation on every new project. That contact doesn't get delegated — it's part of how we keep the standard consistent from year one to year fifteen.

How We Work

Three things we don't
negotiate on

01 — Design First

Design before demolition

Every project is fully designed — materials selected, measurements confirmed, permits filed — before a wall comes down. Changes mid-construction cost twice as much and take three times as long. We prevent that by designing thoroughly upfront.

02 — Transparency

No surprises on cost

Our contracts are fixed-price, not time-and-materials. You know the total before we start. If we discover unexpected conditions — hidden plumbing, asbestos tile, structural issues — we present options and costs before touching anything.

03 — Punch List Discipline

Done means done

A renovation is finished when every punch list item is closed — not when the major work is complete and the small things are "coming back for." We don't close out a project with open items. Our clients don't chase us after completion.
