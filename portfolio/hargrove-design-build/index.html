How It Works

From first call
to final reveal

01 — Discovery

In-Home Consultation

We visit your home, listen to what you want, and walk through the scope together. No sales pitch — just a real conversation about your project.

Common Questions

consultation — answers

A mid-range kitchen remodel in Tampa typically runs $45,000–$85,000. A full luxury renovation with custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, and high-end appliances can reach $100,000–$150,000+. We provide detailed written estimates after an in-home design consultation.
Most kitchen renovations take 6–12 weeks from demo to final walkthrough. Timeline depends on scope, permit processing, and custom cabinet lead times. We provide a detailed project schedule before work begins — and we stick to it.
No. Our in-house crews handle every trade — demolition, framing, plumbing, electrical, tile, cabinetry, and paint. One team, one point of contact. No handoffs, no miscommunication between trades, no gaps in accountability.
We visit your home, listen to how you use the space, and walk through the scope together. We then present floor plans, material samples, and finish options — revising until the design reflects exactly what you want. No pressure, no obligation at the consultation stage.
A master bath renovation in Tampa typically runs $25,000–$60,000 depending on the scope. A full spa-style renovation with walk-in shower, soaking tub, heated floors, and custom vanity can exceed $80,000. We don't shortcut materials to meet a number.
Yes — we manage every permit and inspection from start to finish. Tampa requires permits for structural changes, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work. We handle all of it as part of the project. You never deal with the permit office yourself.
