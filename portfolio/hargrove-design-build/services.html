Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Tampa?

Mid-range kitchen remodels run $45,000–$85,000. A full luxury remodel with custom cabinetry, stone countertops, and high-end appliances reaches $100,000–$150,000+. We provide itemized written estimates after the in-home consultation.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

Typically 6–10 weeks construction time. Add 2–3 weeks for Hillsborough County permitting and 6–8 weeks for custom cabinet lead time — these run concurrently. Total from contract signing to project complete is usually 10–14 weeks.

Do I need permits for a bathroom remodel?

Yes — any work involving plumbing rough-in, electrical, or structural changes requires permits. Hargrove manages all Hillsborough County permit applications and inspections as part of every project, at no extra charge.

Does Hargrove use subcontractors?

Carpentry, tile, painting, and finish work are performed by our in-house crews. Licensed subcontractors are used for plumbing and electrical trade work — pre-vetted by us and supervised by a Hargrove project manager on every visit.
