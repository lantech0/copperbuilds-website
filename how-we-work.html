Build Standards

How we build every site

Not opinions. These are the standards we apply to every home services site we build—drawn from what the top agencies do and what Google rewards.

Dual visitor architecture

Every page serves two visitors at once—the emergency caller who needs a phone number now, and the planned buyer who wants to research first. Both paths are always visible.

Real photos, zero stock

Every site performs better with real team photos, truck shots, and job site images. Authentic images build trust faster and outperform stock photos in local search rankings.

Core Web Vitals mandatory

Every site must pass Google's performance thresholds: LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1, INP under 200ms. These directly affect rankings and call volume—we treat them as hard requirements, not suggestions.

Above-fold formula

The first screen always has a clear service headline, a contact method (phone or form), and a trust signal. Visitors decide in under 3 seconds—we make those 3 seconds count on every page.

4-field max forms

More form fields means fewer completions. We cap all lead capture forms at 4 fields: name, phone, service type, message. Short forms convert at 2–3× the rate of longer ones.

Click-to-call on every page

Phone number in the sticky header, footer, and as a floating button on mobile. The shortest path from "I found them" to a ringing phone—visible on every page, on every device, without scrolling.
