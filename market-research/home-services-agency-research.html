Services Specific to Home Services Clients

These are NOT standard SEO services. These are deliverables that only exist in the home services niche — and what separates agencies that specialize from generalists who just "do SEO."

📍

Service Area Pages (SAPs)

One page per city served: "Plumber in Austin TX," "AC Repair in Round Rock TX." A 10-city contractor needs 10 optimized location pages, each with local keywords, map embed, and LocalBusiness schema. This is the #1 driver of local lead volume for home services.

Must-Have
🚨

Emergency Intent Optimization

Separate landing pages and keyword strategy for "24/7 emergency [service] near me" searches. Emergency callers convert at 3–5× the rate of planned-service searchers — and nobody prices that call correctly unless they're optimized for it.

Must-Have
🏆

Google Local Services Ads (LSA) Management

LSAs appear above everything — above regular Google Ads, above organic results. They come with the "Google Screened" or "Google Guaranteed" badge, which HVAC and plumbing clients trust heavily. Separate from PPC; most generalist agencies don't manage them.

Must-Have
📅

Seasonal Content Strategy

AC tune-up content in March–April. Heating system checks in September–October. Storm damage roofing pages before hurricane season. Home services businesses live and die by seasonal demand — content that misses the timing window generates zero leads.

Must-Have
⭐

Automated Review Generation

SMS + email follow-up sequences triggered after job completion. QR codes on invoices and service receipts. Response templates for Google reviews. Home services live on reviews — "best plumber near me" results are almost entirely driven by review count and recency.

Must-Have
📞

Click-to-Call + Call Tracking

Mobile-first click-to-call buttons on every page and in the header. CallRail integration to track which keyword, page, and campaign generated each call. Home services clients don't fill out forms — they call. If you're not tracking calls, you can't prove SEO ROI.

Must-Have
🗺️

Google Map Pack Domination

The 3-pack (map results) generates 44% of clicks for home services searches. GBP optimization, proximity signals, review velocity, and local citation consistency all drive Map Pack ranking. Some agencies (Contractor Webmasters) have proprietary software (DataPins) just for this.

Must-Have
📸

Before/After Gallery + Project Pages

Photos of real work done — roof replacements, HVAC installs, pipe repairs. These build trust, add local SEO signals (geo-tagged images), and keep visitors on the site longer. Agencies that know home services build gallery functionality into the site architecture.

Nice-to-Have
🤖

AI Receptionist / 24/7 Chat

Scorpion's "Scorpion Connect" and Relentless Digital's "Bullseye Pro" both include AI that answers calls, qualifies leads, and books jobs directly into the contractor's calendar — 24/7. Emergency callers at 2am don't leave voicemails. This feature alone converts lost calls into booked jobs.

New 2025–2026
🔗

Thumbtack / Angi / HomeAdvisor Management

Beyond Google — managing profiles and reviews on lead aggregator platforms where homeowners search before choosing a contractor. Citation consistency across these platforms directly impacts local SEO rankings and lead volume.

Nice-to-Have
📊

ServiceTitan / Jobber CRM Integration

Top agencies (Scorpion, Relentless Digital) connect marketing dashboards directly to the contractor's job-booking software. So the report shows not just "47 calls" but "47 calls → 31 booked jobs → $42,300 in revenue from SEO." This is the future of home services reporting.

New 2025–2026
🏷️

Brand + Truck Wrap Integration

KickCharge Creative is the only agency that explicitly integrates visual branding (truck wraps, uniforms, door hangers) with digital marketing. Brand recognition on service vehicles drives direct searches and builds local brand equity that SEO compounds.

Market Gaps — Where Competitors Fall Short

After analyzing every major home services web + SEO agency in the market, these are the consistent patterns of what they do badly — your openings.

01

$2M+ Revenue Minimum Leaves Small Contractors Unserved

Hook Agency explicitly says they're "best fit for contractors over $2M+." Scorpion and Blue Corona are priced for mid-to-large operations ($3,500–$10,000/mo). The small plumber doing $300K–$800K/year is completely abandoned by the specialists — and forced to use generalist agencies that don't understand their niche.

02

Almost Nobody Publishes Pricing

Of 11 agencies analyzed, only Hook Agency and Built-Right Digital publish actual numbers. Every other niche-specialist hides pricing behind a "free strategy call." Small contractors are already skeptical — they've been burned by marketing agencies before. Transparent pricing wins their trust before the call even happens.

03

Long Contracts Scare Small Contractors Away

Scorpion requires 12 months. Most agencies expect 6-month minimum commitments. A plumber who's been burned by a $2,000/month retainer that delivered zero calls is not signing 12 months with anyone. Month-to-month with performance milestones at an accessible entry price removes the biggest objection in this market.

04

Web Build Speed — Nobody Talks Delivery Time

An HVAC contractor whose site just went down or whose old site isn't generating calls needs revenue now. Not in 12 weeks. Agencies that can deliver a full SEO-ready website in 2–3 weeks — with service area pages, schema, GBP linked, click-to-call, and citations started — have a powerful pitch for urgency-driven contractors.

05

Emergency Intent Pages Are Still Skipped

Even specialist agencies mention emergency SEO but rarely build it as a separate, dedicated deliverable. "24/7 emergency plumber in [city]" and "AC repair emergency [city]" pages need different copy, CTAs, and schema than planned-service pages. The agencies that build these at entry tier will see conversion rates 3–5× higher than those that don't.

06

Reporting Still in Vanity Metrics

Most agencies report rankings and traffic. A plumber doesn't care if they're #4 for "plumber Austin TX" — they care how many calls came in. Call tracking from day one, even at entry tier, shifts reporting from "SEO is working" to "SEO generated 23 calls this month." That's the difference between a client who churns and one who upsells.
