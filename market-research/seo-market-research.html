The 4 Core SEO Pillars

Every US small business web + SEO agency — regardless of size or price — builds their offer on these four pillars. What changes between agencies is how deep they go on each one.

⚙️

Technical SEO

The backend foundation — how well search engines can crawl, read, and index the site.

  • Site speed & Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP)
  • Mobile responsiveness audit
  • Crawlability — robots.txt, XML sitemap
  • Broken links & 404 error resolution
  • HTTPS / SSL verification
  • Redirect management (301 chains)
  • Canonical tags — duplicate content
  • Schema / structured data (JSON-LD)
  • Log file analysis (enterprise only)
  • JavaScript rendering (enterprise only)
📝

On-Page SEO

Content and page-level signals — making each page clearly relevant to its target keyword.

  • Keyword research & intent mapping
  • Title tag optimization
  • Meta description optimization
  • Heading hierarchy (H1–H3)
  • Internal linking strategy
  • Image alt text & file naming
  • Content optimization (LSI, E-E-A-T)
  • Thin/duplicate content fixes
  • URL structure review
  • Content gap analysis
🔗

Off-Page SEO

Authority signals from outside the site — who links to you and how relevant they are.

  • Backlink profile audit
  • White-hat link building outreach
  • Guest posting on relevant sites
  • Digital PR / editorial placements
  • Competitor backlink gap analysis
  • Toxic link audit & disavow
  • Local citation building (NAP)
  • Directory submissions
  • HARO / PR media mentions
  • Resource page link acquisition
📍

Local SEO

Geographic visibility — appearing in Google Maps, "near me" searches, and local directories.

  • Google Business Profile (GBP) setup & optimization
  • Local keyword research ("city + service")
  • NAP consistency across 200+ directories
  • Citation building (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Apple Maps)
  • Review generation strategy
  • Review monitoring & responses
  • Google Map Pack ranking
  • LocalBusiness schema markup
  • GBP posts & photo management
  • Multi-location management (higher tiers)

New Services Added in 2025–2026

Every competitive agency at Tier 2 and above has added these in the last 12 months. If you're not offering them, you're already behind.

🧠

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

Optimizing content so AI tools (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot) cite your pages as answers. Includes structuring content as quotable answer blocks, E-E-A-T signal building, and passage-level optimization so AI extracts your paragraphs directly.

Available at: $1,850+/mo (ClickReady), SmartSites (all tiers), Funnel Boost
🎤

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Specific to voice search and direct answer boxes (Google's "People Also Ask," featured snippets). Structuring individual content pieces so Google pulls them as the definitive answer. Requires FAQ schema, HowTo schema, and clear question-and-answer formatting.

Available at: $1,850+/mo (ClickReady Tier 2+)
✨

AIO — AI Overview Optimization

Google's AI Overviews appear above organic results — if you're not in them, you're invisible on competitive queries. AIO optimization targets appearing inside the AI answer box, not below it. Requires authoritative, structured, source-credible content with clear citation signals.

Available at: $1,850+/mo (ClickReady), Third Marble (mentioned)
📞

Call + Lead Tracking (CallRail)

Moving from "we improved your rankings" to "here are the 47 phone calls SEO generated this month." CallRail integration is now a standard Tier 3 deliverable — tracking call source, duration, and outcome. Agencies that skip this can't prove ROI. It's becoming a client expectation, not a premium.

Standard at: $1,850+/mo · Add-on at lower tiers
🔬

AI Persona Audits

Testing how AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) describe a client's business when asked directly. "What's the best HVAC company in Austin?" — does the client appear? This is new in 2025 and premium agencies are selling it as a Tier 4 differentiator. It's the early-adopter SEO service of 2026.

Premium tier only: $5,500+/mo (ClickReady Market Leader)
📊

E-E-A-T Signal Building

Google's ranking factor: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Agencies now optimize for this explicitly — adding author bios with credentials, linking to expert sources, publishing original research, and building topical authority clusters. Required for medical, legal, financial, and home services.

Now standard at: $1,050+/mo (ClickReady), SmartSites (all tiers)

Market Gaps — Where Competitors Fall Short

Based on live agency pages, these are the consistent weaknesses in the small business SEO agency market right now — and your opportunity to stand out.

01

Pricing Opacity

Only BizIQ, ClickReady, Third Marble, and seoTuners publish actual prices. Everyone else says "contact us." Small business owners are skeptical of hidden pricing — they interpret it as "they'll charge whatever you'll pay." Publishing a real starting price, even a range, builds immediate trust and filters better leads.

02

Web Build + SEO Truly Unified

Most agencies separate "we build sites" from "we do SEO." Even agencies offering both treat them as separate engagements. The client who gets a site built without SEO in mind pays twice — once for the site, again to fix it. Agencies that show how every page is built with schema, speed, and keyword architecture already baked in have a genuine differentiator.

03

GEO/AI Optimization at Starter Tier

GEO, AEO, and AIO optimization only appear at Tier 3 ($1,850+/mo) across all analyzed agencies. Including even basic AI search optimization (FAQ schema, answer-block formatting, E-E-A-T signals) in a Tier 1 or Tier 2 offer creates a "future-proof" positioning that budget agencies can't claim.

04

Reporting Tied to Business Outcomes (Not Just Rankings)

Most entry and mid-tier agencies report on keyword rankings and traffic. Call tracking only appears at $1,850+/mo (ClickReady). Small business owners care about calls and leads — not position #4 for "plumber Austin TX." Delivering even basic call attribution at Tier 2 shows ROI in a language clients understand.

05

Speed of Delivery

Thrive's Premium web build takes 12–20 weeks. Large agencies average 8–16 weeks for a new site. A small agency that delivers a complete, SEO-ready website in 3–5 weeks has a powerful pitch for service businesses who need revenue now, not next quarter.
