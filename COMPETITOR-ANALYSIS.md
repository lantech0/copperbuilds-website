## Top 5 Competitors

### 1. SmartSites
- **URL:** smartsites.com
- **Founded:** 2011 | Paramus, NJ
- **Team size:** ~298 employees
- **Services:** SEO, PPC, Web Design, Social Media, Email/SMS Marketing
- **Positioning:** Full-service performance marketing + web growth partner
- **Strengths:** 1,000+ 5-star reviews, INC5000 fastest-growing, strong brand authority
- **Weakness:** Too broad — not purely small-business focused; high price point
- **Content strategy:** Heavy blog with SEO guides, industry pages, comparison content
- **DA estimate:** 60+

---

### 2. Funnel Boost Media
- **URL:** funnelboostmedia.net
- **Founded:** 2012 | San Antonio, TX
- **Services:** SEO, PPC, Web Design, CRO
- **Positioning:** Search-first web design for small/mid businesses; transparent pricing
- **Strengths:** 4.9-star rating, early GEO adopter, practical fast-ranking sites
- **Weakness:** Regional-leaning; less brand recognition nationally
- **Content strategy:** Comparison pages, local SEO guides, service-specific pages
- **DA estimate:** 40–50

### 3. HigherVisibility
- **URL:** highervisibility.com
- **Positioning:** "Best web design for small businesses" — ranked by major review sites
- **Strengths:** Strong review presence, ranked on Clutch/DesignRush
- **Weakness:** Less content marketing depth
- **DA estimate:** 50+

### 4. Flamingo Agency
