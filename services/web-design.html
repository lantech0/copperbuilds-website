Why most trade websites fail

A website that looks fine but doesn't book jobs is worse than no website.

Slow to load

Half your visitors are on a job site with weak signal. A site that takes 6 seconds to load loses them before they see your number.

Built for desktop first

Most homeowners search for a plumber or electrician from their phone, standing in front of the problem. A cramped mobile layout costs you the call.

No clear next step

A homepage with no phone number above the fold and no quote form makes a visitor hunt for how to reach you. Most won't bother.
