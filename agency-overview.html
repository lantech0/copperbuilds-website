Our process

How we build your site.

No black boxes. No wondering what's happening. Here's exactly what we do, in order, from day one to delivery.

1

Onboarding & Discovery

We start by learning your business. You fill out a short brief: your services, your location, your target customers, and your current situation. We review competitor sites and identify what the top-ranking sites in your niche are doing — so we can match or beat them.

Client brief Competitor review Design reference research
2

Keyword Research

Before writing a single line of code or copy, we research what your customers actually type into Google when they need your service. Every page gets a primary keyword (what it ranks for) and secondary keywords (what supports it). This is the foundation everything else is built on.

SERP analysis Search intent mapping Local keyword variants People Also Ask capture
3

Design & Build

We build mobile-first from scratch — no page builders, no templates. Every section is hand-coded to match the design direction we agreed on. The build is clean, fast, and purpose-built for your trade. You'll see a preview before anything goes live.

Mobile-first HTML/CSS Brand & color system Desktop + mobile screenshots Design review round
4

On-Page SEO

Every page is optimized individually. Title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2 structure, image alt text, canonical tags, and internal linking — all dialed in. The primary keyword for each page appears in the title, the first paragraph, and the main headings. Nothing is left to chance.

Title + meta per page H1/H2/H3 hierarchy Image alt text Canonical + OG tags
5

Schema & Technical SEO

We add structured data (JSON-LD schema) to every page — LocalBusiness, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, and Organization. This tells Google exactly what your business is, where it is, and what it does. We also set up robots.txt and sitemap.xml so Google can crawl everything correctly.

LocalBusiness schema FAQ schema Breadcrumb schema robots.txt + sitemap.xml
6

Performance Optimization

Before the site goes live, we run PageSpeed Insights and verify Core Web Vitals. Mobile performance must score 80+ (we target 90+). Hero images are compressed, lazy loading is enabled, and no layout shift is allowed. A fast site ranks better and converts better — it's not optional.

PageSpeed ≥ 80 mobile LCP < 2.5s CLS < 0.1 Image optimization
7

Quality Gate + Delivery

We run a full pre-launch checklist: every nav link works, no console errors, all forms submit correctly, Google Maps is present on the contact page, and the site looks right on both desktop and mobile. Only after this gate passes does the site go live and get handed to you.

Full link audit Form submission test Cross-device review Client handoff
8

Google Business Profile (Growth & Pro)

For Growth and Pro clients, we also set up or optimize your Google Business Profile. This is what drives your Google Maps listing. We make sure your name, address, and phone exactly match your website — inconsistency here is one of the biggest local SEO killers we see.

NAP consistency Categories + services Photos + first post Review request link

Common questions

Things clients ask before they start.

Do I need to provide photos or copy?

Not necessarily. We research your industry and write copy based on your brief. For photos, we use high-quality stock photography from Pexels. If you have your own photos (especially of your actual work or team), we'll always prefer those — they convert better than stock.

What if I already have a domain?

Great — we build on your domain. You give us the login credentials at the start, we set up hosting, deploy the site, and point your domain to it. You stay in control of the domain at all times.

Can I update the site myself after it's done?

Yes. You receive all the HTML/CSS files. If you're comfortable editing text in a basic code editor, you can make simple changes yourself. For anything structural, we offer one-off edit requests or a Care Plan retainer that covers regular updates.

How long until I start seeing results on Google?

The honest answer: 3–6 months for organic rankings. SEO is not instant — Google needs to crawl, index, and evaluate your site over time. A properly structured site with real keyword targeting and schema will outperform a poorly built one, but it's not overnight. The GBP listing (Google Maps) typically moves faster — sometimes within 2–4 weeks of a verified profile being active.

What happens after the first year of hosting?

Hosting renews at $120/year. You can also move the site to any host you choose — we'll give you the files and help you migrate at no extra charge. You're never forced to stay.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For Growth and Pro packages, we accept a 50% deposit to start and the remaining 50% at delivery (before the site goes live). Contact us to discuss options if you need a different arrangement.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just the onboarding brief (15 minutes of your time) and the deposit. We handle the rest — research, design, build, SEO, and setup. The only time we need you again is when we send the preview for your feedback.

Ready to build something that actually works?

Send us a message and we'll get back to you within 24 hours. No sales pitch — just a quick conversation about your business and what would make the most sense for your situation.
