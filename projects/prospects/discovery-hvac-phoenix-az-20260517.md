## Area Selection Rationale

**Area:** Phoenix, AZ
**Population:** 1.6M+ (metro 5M+)
**Why this sector here:** Phoenix has year-round extreme heat — summer temps regularly exceed 115°F — making HVAC not seasonal but essential. New housing construction in the metro is among the highest in the US. AC unit replacements alone average $6,000–10,000, and service calls are frequent May–September. High demand, high urgency, high average job value.
**Digital adoption gap:** Phoenix HVAC has a sharp split between the dominant players (Parker & Sons, George Brazil, Alaskan Air) who have massive web presence, and hundreds of small owner-operated shops that rely entirely on word-of-mouth with zero digital presence.
**Competition level:** High for large/mid agencies, but the no-website small operator segment is largely unserved by web agencies — these businesses are invisible to agencies targeting established SMBs.
**Expected lead quality:** HOT across all 4 confirmed leads. No-website HVAC in Phoenix = maximum urgency, established businesses, immediate ROI argument.
