## 5. Competitive Positioning

**Market gap to exploit:** Large competitors (SmartSites, WebFX) target mid-to-large businesses. Small agencies like Funnel Boost Media dominate local. Opportunity = **affordable, transparent-pricing, small-business-first positioning** with fast turnaround messaging.

**Differentiators to emphasize:**
- Fixed pricing packages (no hidden costs)
- Fast turnaround (2–4 weeks)
- US-based team
- Industry specialization (restaurants, trades, salons, etc.)
- Included SEO setup with every site
