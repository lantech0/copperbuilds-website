How It Works

From signup to live in 5 days

Every project follows the same battle-tested process. You'll always know what's happening, what's next, and what we need from you.

01 — Discovery Day 1

We learn everything about your business

  • Send you a short onboarding questionnaire (takes ~10 minutes)
  • Review your answers and jump on a 20-minute kickoff call
  • Assign you a dedicated project lead
Your role: Fill out the form, join the call
Project brief locked, timeline confirmed, work begins
02 — Strategy & Planning Day 1–2

Your site blueprint — built before a single pixel is placed

  • Define your site structure — pages, navigation, content hierarchy
  • Research your industry and top local competitors
  • Map your SEO foundation: target keywords, page titles, meta descriptions
  • Plan the conversion funnel — what visitors should do and in what order
Your role: Nothing — this happens in the background
Site blueprint finalized
03 — Design & Build Day 2–3

Your full website built in 48 hours

  • Design and build your full website — mobile-first, responsive
  • Write all on-page copy optimized for search and conversions
  • Integrate your brand colors, logo, and tone of voice
  • Build in full technical SEO: schema markup, sitemap, Open Graph
  • Set up your contact form and lead capture
  • Optimize everything for fast load speed
Your role: Send us your logo and your own photos of your business, team, and work — real images convert far better than stock. If you don't have any yet, we'll use professional stock as a temporary placeholder
Full working website ready for your review
04 — Review & Revisions Day 3–4

You review. We refine. Up to 2 rounds included.

  • Deliver a live preview link — view it on any device
  • Implement all your requested changes within 24 hours
  • Up to 2 rounds of revisions included in every package
Your role: Review on desktop and phone, send one consolidated list of changes, approve the final version
You sign off — site approved for launch
05 — Launch Day 4–5

Go live — and get found on Google from day one

  • Connect your domain and set up SSL (HTTPS)
  • Submit the site to Google Search Console
  • Submit your sitemap for Google indexing
  • Test all pages, links, forms, and load speed
  • Confirm Core Web Vitals pass (LCP, CLS, FID)
Your role: Provide domain credentials, approve go-live
Your site is live and visible on Google
06 — Handover & Support Day 5+

You own the files — and we're still here if you need us

  • Deliver your Site Handover Package: logins, file backups, how-to guide
  • Set up Google Analytics (Growth & Pro plans)
  • Optimize your Google Business Profile (Pro plan)
  • 30-day post-launch support for any bug fixes
  • Monthly performance reports (Growth & Pro plans)
Your role: Review your handover package, start using your new site
You're fully onboarded — ongoing support available whenever you need it
